# ORB V8 INST

This experiment evaluates a deterministic stop condition applied to an Opening Range Breakout framework.

The rule introduces a fixed structural constraint:

If price retraces to 50% of the opening range, the breakout is considered invalid.

The assumption behind the rule is simple.

A valid momentum move should not return deeply into the originating range.

When the midpoint is breached, the structural condition that supported the breakout may no longer exist.

The purpose of the experiment is not to optimize parameters.

The purpose is to observe how a **single deterministic constraint** alters system behavior.
