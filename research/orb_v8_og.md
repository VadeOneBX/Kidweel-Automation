# ORB V8 OG

The OG variant explores a wider structural tolerance compared with the deterministic INST model.

Certain assets exhibit larger volatility envelopes and may invalidate tighter stops prematurely.

This version allows greater price movement before invalidating the breakout condition.

The comparison between OG and INST highlights an important principle:

Small structural rules can materially change system behavior.

The experiment demonstrates how constraints influence outcomes even when the underlying strategy remains unchanged.
