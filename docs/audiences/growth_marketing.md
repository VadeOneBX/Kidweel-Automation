# Decision Systems for Growth & Paid Media

This research began in market microstructure.

The principles generalize to growth systems.

## Environment First

Markets:

- gamma regime

Paid media:

- auction conditions
- demand environment

Signals must be interpreted within environment.

## Constraint Testing

Trading:

- exit constraint

Paid media:

- budget pacing
- attribution window
- spend guardrails

Constraints change outcomes.

## Signal Governance

Trading:

- flow signals

Paid media:

- performance indicators
- conversion signals
- escalation thresholds

Signals must be layered, not isolated.

## Regime Divergence

Same signal. Different environment. Different outcome.

This is true for:

- creative
- channels
- budget
- audiences
